About Concrete Technology Tools

Concrete Technology Tools is a web-based platform developed to make repetitive concrete technology calculations and formulation-related workflows easier to manage.

The platform is intended for concrete technologists, admixture professionals, RMC engineers, laboratory professionals, civil engineers and students working with concrete materials and mix development.

The objective is to bring practical engineering tools into one accessible web application rather than relying on disconnected spreadsheets and manual calculations.

Concrete Mix Design & Admixture Technology

Tools and workflows focused on practical concrete engineering and laboratory development.

Concrete Mix Design

Concrete mix design involves selecting and proportioning cementitious materials, water, fine aggregate, coarse aggregate and chemical admixtures to achieve the required concrete performance. A systematic mix design workflow helps engineers evaluate material proportions, water-cement ratio, workability and other project requirements.

IS 10262 Concrete Mix Design

IS 10262 provides guidelines for concrete mix proportioning in India. Concrete engineers and RMC professionals commonly use mix-design calculations together with laboratory trials and material-specific adjustments to develop practical concrete mixes.

Concrete Admixture Formulation

Chemical admixture formulation can involve evaluating different raw materials, PCE components, dosage levels, workability and retention behaviour. Trial-based analysis can help concrete technologists develop formulations for specific materials and performance requirements.

Concrete Workability and Retention

Workability and retention are important considerations when evaluating fresh concrete, particularly for RMC applications where concrete may need to maintain the required workability during transportation, placing and finishing.
