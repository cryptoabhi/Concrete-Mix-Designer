Concrete Mix Designer

Design concrete mixes using an engineering-based workflow with provisions for concrete grade, materials, aggregate proportions, water-cement ratio and mix calculations. The tool is developed around the principles of IS 10262 concrete mix design.

About Concrete Technology Tools

Concrete Technology Tools is developed to make repetitive concrete technology calculations and formulation-related workflows easier to manage.

The platform is intended for concrete technologists, admixture professionals, RMC engineers, laboratory professionals, civil engineers and students who work with concrete materials and mix development.

The objective is to bring practical engineering tools into one accessible web application rather than relying on disconnected spreadsheets and manual calculations.
